Meto · Chat (Vato style)

Design draft 5 · Rose Night · Tabs = Option B · Chats list + Chat ke andar · Rose + Gold theme

Vato se kya liya, kya badla

  • Liya: 4 tabs (Chats · Friend · Followed · Call Log), upar Official Notice card, har chat alag rounded card, laal unread badge, online dot, side me floating offer icon.
  • Badla: white ki jagah premium dark + rose-gold, verified ✓ aur Level badge, golden ring (jinka naya status hai), typing… aur seen ✓✓.
  • Friend = dono ne ek doosre ko follow kiya → seedha Chat / Call. Followed = jinhone aapko follow kiya → "Follow" back button (NEW tag naye followers pe).
  • Call Log filter: All (saari calls) · Missed (aapko aayi lekin uthayi nahi, laal count ke saath) · Called (jo calls aapne ki, chahe uthi ho ya nahi). Missed tab kholte hi count 0 ho jaata hai.
  • Offer icon: sirf tab dikhega jab asli offer chal raha ho. Vato jaisa nakli countdown nahi lagayenge (Play Store aur users dono ke liye galat).
  • Chat screen (andar) wahi rahega jo pichle design me tha (text, photo, gift, video call button).
