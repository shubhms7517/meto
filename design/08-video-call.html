Meto · Video Call

Design draft 2 · premium backgrounds · Calling · Incoming · In call · Call ended · Rose Night theme

Kaise kaam karega

  • Calling: saamne wale ki photo + pulse rings, "Calling…", per-minute rate. Peeche aapka camera blur me. 30 sec me na uthaya to "No answer" (missed call ban jaayegi).
  • Incoming: full-screen, ringtone + vibrate. Accept (hara, hilta hua) / Decline. Quick replies: "Can't talk now" seedha chat me jaata hai.
  • Call chal rahi: poori screen pe saamne wala, kone me aapka chhota video (drag + flip). Upar timer, coin balance + kitne minute bache. Balance kam hone pe "2 min left · Recharge". Neeche: Mute, Camera off, End, Speaker, Gift.
  • Call khatam: kitni der, kitne coins, kaun se gifts, rating (1–5 ⭐, admin ke liye quality), Call again / Message.
  • Coins: server har minute kaatega. Coins khatam → call apne aap band. (Wallet phase tak calls free/test mode me chalengi.)
  • Safety: blocked user call nahi kar sakta, call me ⋯ → Report, aur pehli call pe "Respect & safety" reminder.
