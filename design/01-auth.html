Meto · Login & Registration

Design draft 1 · Rose + Gold premium theme · Screens 1–5

Flow

  1. Splash → app check karta hai ki user pehle se logged in hai ya nahi.
  2. Logged in hai → seedha Home. Nahi hai → Welcome.
  3. Phone → OTP. Naya number ho to registration, purana ho to login. Alag signup page ki zaroorat nahi.
  4. Naye user ke liye Profile setup (photo, naam, gender, birthday, 18+ confirm). Iske baad Home.
  5. Google login → agar profile adhoora hai to Profile setup, warna Home.
