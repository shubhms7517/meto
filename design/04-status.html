Meto · Status

Design draft 1 · Favourites row + Status (24 hours) · Rose + Gold theme

Kaise kaam karega

  • Favourites row: sabse pehle "Your status +", phir jinhe aap follow karte ho. Rose-gold ring = naya status (abhi dekha nahi), grey ring = dekh liya, bina ring = status nahi. Green dot = online. Order: naya status → online → baaki.
  • Status dekhna: full screen photo, upar progress lines (har status 5 second), right tap = agla, left tap = pichla, daba ke rakho = ruk jaao, neeche swipe = band. Neeche reply (seedha chat me jaata hai), ❤ react, aur 📹 video call.
  • Status lagana: gallery/camera se photo, caption, aur kaun dekh sake: Everyone ya Followers only. 24 ghante baad apne aap gayab.
  • Apna status: kitne logon ne dekha, kisne ❤ kiya / reply kiya, kitna time bacha, aur Delete.
  • Safety (pehle din se): har status pe ⋯ → Report, photos ko server pe compress + GPS hataana (album jaisa), ek din me max 10 status.
